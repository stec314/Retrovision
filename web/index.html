Retrovision probe firmware

Flash the probe (XIAO ESP32-S3 or classic ESP32 / NodeMCU-32S) from your browser over USB. Nothing leaves your computer.

1. Connect

Plug the board in with a data-capable USB cable. On a XIAO ESP32-S3, use the USB-C port. On a classic ESP32 board you may need the CP210x or CH340 driver, and to hold BOOT while the flasher connects.

This browser has no Web Serial. Use Chrome, Edge or Opera on desktop, or flash from the Retrovision Android app.

2. Flash

Web Serial is not available in this browser. Web Serial needs HTTPS.

If the board is not detected

Hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, then try again. The board then enters the ROM bootloader and always appears as a serial port.
